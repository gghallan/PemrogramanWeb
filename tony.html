Curse Of Lottery.

If someone handed you your dream body right now, would you know how to maintain it?
Or if you were given a million dollars, would you know how to grow it? 

        The thing that you want isn't the point.
        It's who you have to become in order to get the thing that you want. That is the whole point.
        And you don't become that person by rushing through the process.
        You become that person by devoting yourself to the things that you're currently trying to skip and bypass.
